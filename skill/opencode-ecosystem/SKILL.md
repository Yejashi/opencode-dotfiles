# OpenCode community ecosystem

An index of the [awesome-opencode](https://github.com/awesome-opencode/awesome-opencode)
registry, split by category. Use it to answer "has someone already built this?"
before writing a plugin or wrapper.

**These entries are third-party and unvetted.** The registry lists projects; it
does not audit them. Treat a hit as a lead to go read the repo, not as a
recommendation to install.

Regenerate with `~/.config/opencode/bin/skills-import-awesome`, then
`~/.config/opencode/bin/skills-build`.
